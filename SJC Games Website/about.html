ABOUT

WHO WE ARE

SJC Games is an independent game development studio focused on creating original games with memorable gameplay and unique ideas.

OUR GOAL

Our goal is simple: create games that players genuinely enjoy playing, while building a studio capable of producing exciting and ambitious projects.

INDEPENDENT

SJC Games is an independent studio. We want the freedom to experiment, create our own ideas and develop games without losing what makes them unique.

WHAT DOES SJC STAND FOR?

SJC stands for:

Snake
Jaguar
Capybara

These three animals represent the idea behind SJC Games and are part of the identity of the studio.
