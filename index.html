More About Us

SJC stands for Snake, Jaguar and Capybara. The name represents the people and ideas behind SJC Games.

SJC Games was created by James Harry Bernard Wilkinson. James started developing games on Roblox at the age of 11.

After six years of game development, James began working on a game for Steam. The game didn't do as well as hoped, but the experience helped inspire the next step.

James then gathered friends together and created SJC Games.

Today, SJC Games is focused on creating original games and building projects that the team can be proud of.
